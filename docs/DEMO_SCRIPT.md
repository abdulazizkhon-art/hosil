# Demo video script (about 3 minutes)

## Before you record
0. On Streamlit the demo opens in **Split screen**: restaurant on the left, farmer on the right, already in sync. Steps 3–4 below are then not needed. Use the role switcher at the top of either pane to open Hosil HQ.
1. Open the demo (see README) in Chrome. Use one browser profile: both windows must share the same data.
2. Click **Reset demo data** (bottom of the sidebar) so every number starts clean.
3. Make **two windows side by side**: left = `…/index.html#/restaurant`, right = `…/index.html#/farmer`. A third tab with `#/admin` is for the end.
4. Zoom to 90% if the window is narrower than 1400 px (Ctrl/Cmd and −).
5. Hide the bookmarks bar, close other tabs, turn on Do Not Disturb.

## Backup moves if something goes wrong
- A toast didn't appear → click the bell (top right); everything is also in Contracts.
- You signed the wrong contract → it still works as a demo; or **Reset demo data** and redo from shot 3.
- Typing your own AI request also works, e.g. `150 kg onions weekly under 4000 so'm` or `30 kg kashnich twice a week`.
